YOUR JOURNEY STARTS HERE

Jeep Batur Horizon takes you across the volcanic highlands of Kintamani, from the still-dark road up to Mount Batur's sunrise point to the hardened black lava fields shaped by the 1963 eruption. It is a way to meet this landscape without the two-hour hike, guided by drivers who grew up on this mountain.

Every route is run in a private open-top 4WD Jeep, timed around the sunrise and paced by your group, not a fixed tour schedule.
